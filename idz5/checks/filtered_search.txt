=== 4.4 Поиск с фильтрацией по атрибутам ===

Запрос:
SELECT id, title, price, rating
FROM products
WHERE MATCH('laptop') AND price BETWEEN 30000 AND 80000 AND rating >= 4.0
ORDER BY rating DESC
LIMIT 10;
